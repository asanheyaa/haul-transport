Frequently Asked Questions

HIG Transport (Haul It Girl Transport) is a woman-owned based business offering premium chauffeur and delivery service to locals and tourists in the Las Vegas metropolitan area.

We provide both personal driver, comfort rides, and express delivery service including seniors, outpatient or post surgery transport, packages, errands, groceries, and documents with exception to weapons or illegal and contraband items or substances.

Children must be at least 18 years old to have their own Verified Rider Account and ride alone. Children under 13 must be accompanied by an adult, and for young children, parents should bring their own car or booster seat.

Nevada law (NRS 484B.157) requires children under 6 years old AND less than 57 inches (4 feet 9 inches) tall to be secured in an approved child restraint system, such as a car seat or booster seat. Children under 2 must be in a rear-facing seat.
