The Ultimate Guide to Las Vegas Airport Transfers

Mirhayot
January 15, 2025

Arriving at Harry Reid International Airport can be both exciting and overwhelming. With millions of visitors passing through each year, having a reliable transportation plan is essential for starting your Las Vegas trip on the right foot.

Why Pre-Book Your Airport Transfer?

One of the biggest mistakes travelers make is waiting until they land to figure out transportation. Ride-share surge pricing during peak hours can triple your expected fare, and taxi lines can stretch for 30 minutes or more during busy periods.

By pre-booking a private transfer, you lock in your rate, skip the lines, and have a professional driver waiting for you at arrivals. Your driver will track your flight in real time, so even if your plane is delayed, they'll be there when you land.

What to Expect from a Private Transfer

When you book with Elogwnt Transport, your experience begins the moment you step off the plane. Our drivers meet you at the designated pickup area with a personalized sign. From there, you'll be escorted to a clean, comfortable Cadillac SUV with climate control, bottled water, and phone chargers.

The drive from the airport to the Las Vegas Strip typically takes 10-15 minutes, depending on traffic. Heading to downtown? That's usually around 15-20 minutes. We also serve Henderson, Summerlin, and other surrounding areas.

Tips for a Smooth Airport Pickup

  • Share your flight number when booking so we can track arrivals
  • Have your luggage count ready so we can ensure enough cargo space
  • Keep your phone charged and ringer on for driver communication
  • Allow a few extra minutes for customs if arriving on an international flight

Whether you're visiting for business or pleasure, starting your trip with a professional, stress-free transfer sets the tone for an amazing Las Vegas experience.
